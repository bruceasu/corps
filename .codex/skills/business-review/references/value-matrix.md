# Value Matrix

| Value Dimension | Strong Evidence | Weak Evidence | Review Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Increase revenue | Direct conversion, retention, trading, deposit, AUM, or paid product impact | Generic growth claim | What revenue path changes? |
| Reduce risk | Prevents financial loss, compliance exposure, fraud, operational errors, or unauthorized access | Vague safety improvement | What risk event becomes less likely or less severe? |
| Reduce labor cost | Replaces repeated manual work with measurable frequency and duration | One-off convenience | How many hours per month are saved? |
| Improve decision efficiency | Speeds up concrete operational decisions | Passive information display | Which decision becomes faster or better? |
| Satisfy regulatory/audit need | Tied to explicit rule, audit finding, or mandatory control | Nice-to-have traceability | What requirement or audit gap is addressed? |
| Improve customer experience | Reduces customer delay, complaint, failure, or confusion | Internal-only preference | What customer pain is reduced? |
